GTFS Validator terms

GTFS Validator provides synchronous GTFS Schedule validation and analysis as an experimental service powered by GTFS Analyzer.

Results are informational and should be reviewed before operational or publication decisions. Requests may be rejected when a feed exceeds the service’s size, time, concurrency, or resource limits.

You must have the right to submit an uploaded feed or request analysis of a public feed URL. Do not submit credentials, private URLs, or data that you are not authorized to process.
